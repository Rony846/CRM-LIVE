Refund & Returns

Last updated: June 2026

Returns

Unused products in original packaging may be returned within 7 days of delivery. Products showing a manufacturing defect on arrival are eligible for free replacement.

How to start a return

Contact us at service@musclegrid.in or 099990 36254 with your order number and reason. We arrange pickup where serviceable.

Refunds

Once the returned item is received and inspected, approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. For COD orders, refunds are made by bank transfer.

Non-returnable

Installed products, physically damaged items, and made-to-order/custom units are non-returnable except under warranty.
