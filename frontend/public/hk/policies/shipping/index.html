Shipping Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Coverage & charges

We ship worldwide from Hong Kong. All prices include customs & import duties — your order is delivered to your door with nothing extra to pay on arrival (Delivered Duty Paid). Free door delivery.

Dispatch & delivery

Orders are typically dispatched within 24–48 hours. Delivery takes 5–7 business days depending on location. You'll receive tracking once your order ships.

Installation

For inverters and batteries, our team coordinates professional installation after delivery. We'll email you to schedule a convenient time.

Delays

Remote locations or unforeseen events may add time. Contact us anytime for a status update.
