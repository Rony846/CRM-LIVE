About MuscleGrid

MuscleGrid Hong Kong supplies power-solution products — solar inverters, LiFePO₄ lithium batteries and mainline voltage stabilizers. Every order ships worldwide from Hong Kong with all customs & import duties included, delivered to your door with nothing extra to pay.

MuscleGrid started offline operations in October 2021, reaching local markets. After an overwhelming response, we moved online to reach thousands of customers. Every product is engineered and assembled in India, and now ships duty-paid worldwide from Hong Kong — with all customs & import duties already included in the price.
