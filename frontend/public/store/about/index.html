About MuscleGrid

MuscleGrid is a trademark of Electronics Bay. We are a manufacturer of India's power-solution products — solar inverters, LiFePO₄ lithium batteries and mainline voltage stabilizers — engineered for Indian homes, shops and industry.

MuscleGrid started offline operations in October 2021, reaching local markets. After an overwhelming response, we moved online to reach thousands of customers across India. Every product is designed and assembled with pride in India.
