## 4. Orders, Pricing & Payment

### 4.1 Order Placement
All purchase orders shall be raised exclusively through the **MuscleGrid Dealer Portal**. Verbal orders, WhatsApp messages, emails and field-team commitments do not constitute binding orders until reflected in the CRM as a confirmed Proforma Invoice (PI).

### 4.2 Pricing
- Dealer Net Prices are published on the CRM and may be revised by MuscleGrid at any time without prior notice. Orders are billed at the price prevailing on the date of PI generation, **not** the date of indent.
- All prices are **ex-warehouse, exclusive of GST and freight**, unless an order explicitly states otherwise.
- Special/scheme prices, quantity discounts and seasonal offers — if any — are governed by separate scheme circulars and are non-cumulative unless stated.

### 4.3 Payment Terms
- The default payment term is **100% advance against PI**, unless a written credit limit has been sanctioned to the Dealer.
- Credit, where extended, is at MuscleGrid's sole discretion, secured by a post-dated cheque / bank guarantee / personal guarantee as required, and is subject to periodic review.
- **Default in payment** beyond the credit period attracts **interest @ 18% per annum (1.5% per month)** on the overdue amount, calculated from the invoice due date, and entitles MuscleGrid to immediately suspend further dispatches and recover stock.
- Cheque dishonour will attract a flat **₹2,500 + GST** processing fee plus all bank charges, and may invite criminal action under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.

### 4.4 Title & Risk
- Title in the goods passes to the Dealer only upon **full realisation** of payment.
- Risk in the goods passes at the moment of handover to the freight carrier at MuscleGrid's warehouse. The Dealer is responsible for insuring transit if desired.

### 4.5 Returns & Cancellations
- Confirmed PIs cannot be cancelled once dispatch has been initiated.
- Returns are accepted only for (a) manifest manufacturing defects reported within 7 days of receipt with photo/video evidence, or (b) wrong dispatch on MuscleGrid's part.
- Buy-back / sale-or-return arrangements are not offered. Slow-moving stock at the dealer's premises is the dealer's commercial risk.

## 5. Warranty & Spare-Parts Support

### 5.1 MuscleGrid's Obligations
MuscleGrid commits to the Dealer that it will:

(a) **Honour all Product warranties** as per the warranty card printed and packed with each Product. Standard warranty currently is 24 months for batteries, 36 months for inverters, 12 months for stabilizers, and 25 years performance + 10 years product warranty for solar panels — refer to the SKU-level warranty matrix on the CRM, which is binding.

(b) **Supply spare parts at dealer net prices** for the duration of the warranty period plus an additional **five (5) years** thereafter, subject to commercial availability. Spare parts orders are placed through the CRM's "Service Request" workflow and shipped within **5 working days** of PI clearance, freight at MuscleGrid's cost for in-warranty replacements and at the Dealer's cost for out-of-warranty.

(c) **Provide a documented service manual, fault-diagnosis guide and exploded-view diagrams** for each Product family on the CRM, refreshed when the design revision changes.

(d) **Conduct dealer-technician training** at MuscleGrid's facility or via video, at no cost to the dealer (other than the dealer's own travel/lodging), at least once per calendar year. The Dealer is required to nominate at least one trained technician.

(e) **Process warranty claims within 7 working days** of receiving complete documentation (serial number, purchase invoice, fault photo/video, customer details) through the CRM. Approved claims will be settled via free-replacement spare or credit note as appropriate.

(f) **Maintain an in-warranty defective-stock collection mechanism** through the CRM's reverse-pickup workflow.

### 5.2 Dealer's After-Sales Obligations
The Dealer is the **first line of after-sales support** for every End Customer in its Territory. Specifically, the Dealer shall:

(a) **Acknowledge customer complaints within 4 hours** of receipt during working hours; provide a first-visit / first-response within **48 hours** for urban locations and **72 hours** for semi-urban/rural.

(b) **Maintain a minimum spare-parts buffer stock** of fast-moving SKUs as advised by MuscleGrid from time to time — typically PCBs, fans, fuses, terminals, MOSFETs and battery posts for the top-selling models.

(c) **Capture every warranty / service interaction in the CRM** within 24 hours, with the Product serial number, customer contact, fault description and resolution. Service interactions not logged in the CRM are deemed not to have occurred for warranty-pass-through purposes.

(d) **Not refuse service** on the grounds that the customer purchased the Product from another dealer, marketplace or location. After-sales support is a brand obligation, not a per-sale obligation. The Dealer's territory-wide service obligation is consideration for being appointed an authorised dealer.

(e) **Pass through MuscleGrid's warranty to the End Customer** without dilution. The Dealer shall not offer shorter warranties, charge for in-warranty visits, or substitute non-MuscleGrid parts.

(f) **Refer escalations** that cannot be resolved at the Dealer's level to MuscleGrid's central service helpdesk via the CRM within the same working day.

### 5.3 Charge-back for Wrongful Claims
If MuscleGrid's quality lab determines that a returned Product (a) has been tampered with, (b) shows physical damage from mishandling, (c) shows installation in non-permissible environments (deep cycling on flat plate, over-voltage charging, exposure to flooding), or (d) is from a counterfeit batch — the Dealer will be debited for the full MRP plus 18% handling charges, and the Product returned at the Dealer's freight cost.

The MuscleGrid brand is the most valuable asset jointly relied upon by both parties. The Dealer shall:

(a) Display MuscleGrid signage, posters and product literature prominently at the Dealer's premises in accordance with MuscleGrid's brand guidelines (provided separately, updated on the CRM).

(b) Use the MuscleGrid name, logo, trade marks and product names **only in the form, colour, font and proportion** prescribed by MuscleGrid. No alteration, abbreviation, translation, or co-branding with the dealer's own name is permitted without written approval.

(c) Not register, attempt to register, or use as a domain name, social-media handle, app name or trading style any name, mark or device that is identical, similar to, or that incorporates "MuscleGrid", "MG", "Muscle Grid" or any phonetic equivalent. All such intellectual property vests exclusively with MuscleGrid.

(d) Surrender all signage, point-of-sale material and brand collateral within 30 days of termination of this Agreement.

### 6.2 Pricing & Market Conduct
(a) **Not sell below MRP** in a manner that constitutes predatory pricing, dumping or distress sale. Discount limits, if any, will be circulated periodically.

(b) **Not list MuscleGrid products on online marketplaces** (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, IndiaMart, etc.) without specific written authorisation from MuscleGrid. MuscleGrid maintains a separate D2C/marketplace channel; unauthorised marketplace listing by a dealer is a material breach.

(c) **Not engage in cross-territorial dumping** — i.e., aggressively selling outside the Dealer's primary Territory at distress prices to undercut another authorised dealer.

(d) **Not sell to other resellers, sub-dealers, hawkers or unorganised traders** who are not the End Customer, unless specifically appointed as a Sub-Dealer with MuscleGrid's written consent.

### 6.3 Customer Communication
(a) **Not disparage** MuscleGrid, its Products, its other channel partners, or competitor brands in any customer-facing communication, social-media post, review platform, or trade press.

(b) **Not make claims** about the Product (efficiency, backup hours, certifications, BIS rating, IP rating) that are not in MuscleGrid's published datasheets.

(c) **Direct unresolved customer escalations** to MuscleGrid's central helpdesk and refrain from making public commitments (refund, replacement, free upgrade) on MuscleGrid's behalf without prior approval.

(d) **Use only original MuscleGrid packaging, manuals and warranty cards** when handing over a Product to the End Customer.

### 6.4 Counterfeit & Grey Market

Discovery of any such activity is grounds for **immediate termination, criminal complaint** under the Trade Marks Act, 1999 and the Copyright Act, 1957, and forfeiture of all security deposits and pending payouts.

## 7. Sales Targets, Upselling & Performance

### 7.1 Quarterly Targets
(a) MuscleGrid will communicate quarterly sales targets via the CRM, segmented by Product category. Targets are revised based on past performance, seasonal patterns and Territory potential.

(b) Achievement of targets unlocks **slab-based incentives, marketing co-op funding and credit-limit enhancements**. The current incentive grid is published on the CRM.

(c) **Sustained under-performance** — defined as achievement below 60% of target for three (3) consecutive quarters — will result in (i) review of Territory assignment, (ii) reduction of credit limit, and (iii) potentially termination of dealer status.

### 7.2 Active Selling & Upsell Obligations
The Dealer is expected to be a **value-adding, upselling channel partner**, not a passive order-taker. Specifically:

(a) **Up-sell to higher-capacity / higher-warranty SKUs** where the End Customer's application justifies it. Selling a 150Ah battery where a 220Ah is suited, purely to hit a lower price point, harms both the customer experience and the brand.

(b) **Cross-sell the full range**: a battery sale should attempt to include an inverter, stabilizer, or solar add-on. A solar enquiry should attempt to include charge controllers and batteries. Dealer-level monthly category-attach ratios will be tracked on the CRM and reviewed quarterly.

(c) **Capture leads in the CRM** — every walk-in, telephonic enquiry and field-visit must be logged with the customer's name, contact, requirement and probable timeline. Unlogged leads cannot be claimed for incentive eligibility.

(d) **Convert quotes within validity** — quoted prices are valid for 7 days from PI generation. The Dealer is responsible for closing or escalating quotes before they expire.

(e) **Participate in MuscleGrid-led campaigns** (festive offers, exchange programs, society-society activations, builder tie-ups) with the same effort given to the Dealer's own sales activities.

### 7.3 Reporting
(a) The Dealer shall maintain on the CRM, at the Dealer's own effort, **real-time stock positions, customer purchase records (with serial numbers) and warranty registrations**.

(b) MuscleGrid retains the right to **audit** dealer premises, books, stock-cards and CRM logs with reasonable notice. Refusal of audit is a material breach.

## 12. Term & Termination

12.1 This Agreement is effective from the date of Dealer onboarding on the CRM and remains in force for an initial period of **twelve (12) months**, auto-renewing for further twelve-month periods unless terminated.

12.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving **thirty (30) days' written notice** through the CRM.

12.3 MuscleGrid may terminate this Agreement with **immediate effect**, without notice or compensation, on occurrence of any of the following:
- Material breach not cured within 7 days of written notice
- Insolvency, winding up, NCLT petition or composition with creditors of the Dealer
- Cheque dishonour, criminal proceedings, or police FIR against the Dealer or its signatories
- Counterfeit, grey-market, or trade-mark infringement activity
- Sale on unauthorised online marketplaces
- Sustained under-performance per Clause 7.1(c)

- Breach of confidentiality or data-protection obligations

(a) The Dealer shall immediately cease using all MuscleGrid marks, return all collateral, and remove all signage within 30 days.
(b) All outstanding dues from the Dealer become immediately payable, regardless of original credit terms.
(c) MuscleGrid will, at its option, buy back un-opened, current-generation stock at the **lower of the original invoice price or current dealer-net price, less 15% restocking**, and within 60 days subject to physical verification.
(d) Warranty obligations to End Customers already sold by the Dealer continue to be honoured by MuscleGrid directly or via a successor dealer; the outgoing Dealer agrees to hand over customer records and pending service tickets through the CRM.
(e) Clauses on Confidentiality (8), IP (9), Indemnity (11), Dispute Resolution (13) and Survival (14) survive termination.
